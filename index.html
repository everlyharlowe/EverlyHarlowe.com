✦   About   ✦

The Writer
Behind the Realm

Everly Harlowe writes adult dark fantasy romance for readers who want their hearts broken beautifully. Her debut series, The Lyndere Chronicles, is a world built in the quiet hours—a place where magic is feared in one realm and worshipped in another, and where two people from opposite sides of that divide are destined to change everything.

When she isn't writing, she's deep in the lore she's building: tracing maps of mist-shrouded towns, naming creatures that sense emotion, and plotting the kind of slow-burn romance that keeps you up far too late.

The Chronicles are just beginning.

✦   Lore   ✦

The World of
the Chronicles

The Mortal Realm

Greyveil

A bleak, mist-shrouded world where magic is not merely distrusted—it is hunted. Those born with power live hidden lives or pay with their blood.

Magic feared  ·  Practitioners killed  ·  Eternal mist

The Magical Realm

Lyndere (Lin-DEER)

Temperate and luminescent, Lyndere is beautiful in the way a blade is beautiful—it will take your breath away and it will cut you. Magic is worshipped here, woven into every living thing. But something is fracturing, and the two realms are merging.

Luminescent  ·  Magical  ·  Merging crisis

The Five Forces

Magic of the Realm

Magic in Lyndere flows through five fundamental forces: emotion, dreams and the subconscious, music and sound, memory and time, and creatures and beasts.

Emotion  ·  Dreams  ·  Music  ·  Memory  ·  Beasts

The Threat

The Vorrkai & Malabriel

The Vorrkai are predatory beings with iridescent skin and absorptive magic that feeds on others. Bound in shadow, their ancient leader seeks to tear open the seals between realms.

Absorptive magic  ·  Exiled  ·  Seal-breaker
